## Overview

Searching for your biological parents is simple in Parent Search.

## Steps

1. Sign in to your Parent Search account.
2. Click on "Start New Search".
3. Enter any known details (birth date, city, etc).
4. Upload optional documents for better results.
5. Submit your search request.

Results may take a few days. You’ll receive notifications by email.
