---
title: Submitting a DNA Test
description: How to upload and submit your DNA test results.
---

## Overview

You can link your DNA test to improve search accuracy.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to DNA Section">
    Navigate to "My Account" > "DNA Tests".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload DNA Data">
    ````
    Select your DNA raw data file (e.g., .txt or .zip) and upload.
    Only official test results are accepted.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for Review">
    ````
    Click "Submit". You’ll get a confirmation email when the DNA test is linked.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
