## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

<Callout kind="info">
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- A stable internet connection
- Access to your email for account verification
</Callout>

No software installation required. Everything runs in your browser.

## Account Setup

Start by creating an account or logging in to the Parent Portal.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="New Users" icon="user-plus">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Visit the Join Page" icon="external-link">
        Go to [https://parentsearch.org/join](https://parentsearch.org/join).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter Your Details" icon="edit-3">
        Provide your name, email, and create a secure password. Agree to the terms.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
        Check your inbox and click the verification link sent to your email.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Returning Users" icon="log-in">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Go to Parent Portal" icon="globe">
        Navigate to [https://parentsearch.org/login](https://parentsearch.org/login).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign In" icon="key">
        Enter your email and password. Use two-factor if enabled.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Create Your First Time Capsule

Once logged in as a parent, build your time capsule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start New Capsule" icon="plus">
    Click the "Create Time Capsule" button on the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Content" icon="image">
    Upload photos, record a video message, or write a letter. Include memories and messages for your child.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Details" icon="tag">
    Enter your child's potential search terms like first name or city.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Secure" icon="save">
    Review and save. Your capsule is now private and ready for discovery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Capsules are encrypted and only accessible when your child searches with matching details.
</Callout>

## Perform an Initial Search

If you're searching for a parent's capsule:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Search" icon="search">
    From the homepage, enter your parent's name or partial last name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow Results" icon="filter">
    Optionally refine by city or other details on the next screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Matches" icon="eye">
    Review potential matches and request access if ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>
